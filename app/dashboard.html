What the words mean

  • Risk score: the system's estimate of how likely a payment is to be fraud, from 0% (certainly genuine) to 100% (certainly fraud).
  • Held for review: the payment is paused until a person checks it. It does not mean the payment is fraud; most held payments in real use turn out to be fraud, but not all.
  • Why bigger payments are held at lower risk: missing a fraud costs the whole payment, while a needless check costs about €5 of staff time. So a €1,000 payment is held at about 0.5% risk, but a €5 payment only at about 50%.
  • Practice payments: examples from past data that contain far more fraud than normal, used to show the dashboard working.
